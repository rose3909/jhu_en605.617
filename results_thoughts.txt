Results Discussion

GPU performance improved as the number of launched threads and available blocks increased. 
With 512 threads in two blocks, the branchless GPU kernel took 9.103 ms and was slower than the CPU’s 5.943 ms 
because the GPU was substantially underutilized. This portion took me awhile to realize and I spent quite a bit of time
trying to figure it out!
At 2,048 threads, the branchless GPU time decreased to 2.445 ms, approximately 2.85 times faster than the corresponding 
CPU time of 6.957 ms. Branching increased GPU execution time by approximately 9.5% in the 1,024-thread configuration 
and 10.8% in the 2,048-thread configuration, which is what I expected to see. 

Also, one thing I found was that CPU measurements varied between runs even though even though the CPU algorithm processed 
the same 8,388,608 values each time and was unaffected by the CUDA launch configuration.
